About

The world ended. You are the Overseer: the one who keeps an underground shelter running and its survivors alive. By day you build and merge rooms, put people to work where their stats fit, and fortify the yard above. By night the horde comes. Hold the shelter from below and let your defenses work, or step outside, take a gun and fight at the door yourself.

Features

  • Two views, one place: a side-on shelter cutaway and a top-down yard, one Tab away.
  • Build, merge and upgrade rooms; match survivors' stats to jobs.
  • Living survivors with traits, moods, friendships, rivalries and children.
  • Create your own Overseer with a background and 10 perks to rank up.
  • Fires, leaks, surges, rats and sickness below; strangers (and bites) at the door.
  • Nights you can plan for: zombie routes are shown all day, so bend them with barricades and build a kill zone.
  • Six upgradeable guns, from pistol to LMG.
