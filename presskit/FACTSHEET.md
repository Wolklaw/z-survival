## Short description

Run an underground shelter by day, defend its door by night.

## Long description

The world ended. You are the **Overseer**: the person who keeps a cutaway shelter running and
the survivors inside it alive. By day you build and merge rooms, put people to work where their
stats fit best, and fortify the yard above. By night the horde comes, and you either hold the
shelter from below and let your defenses do the work, or step outside, take a gun, and fight at
the door yourself.

Bright, chunky cartoon art over a scavenged, apocalyptic world. Readable at a glance, warm inside, dangerous outside.

## Features

- **Two views, one place.** A side-on cutaway of the shelter and a top-down yard above it. Tab flips between them; both keep running.
- **Shelter management.** Power, food and water rooms, storage, medbay, workshop, radio, armory, gym, classroom and lounge. Same-type rooms merge into bigger, more efficient ones. Upgrade rooms across three levels.
- **Living survivors.** Every survivor has stats, traits and a mood with reasons. Friendships and rivalries form, couples can have children, stats grow with use or in training rooms.
- **Your Overseer.** Create and name your own: pick a look and a background (Engineer, Medic, Quartermaster, Scavenger or Soldier) with a starting edge. Earn XP and spend perk points across 10 perks.
- **Trouble below.** Fires, leaks, power surges, rats and sickness break out. Send someone with the right stat to fix them. Bites turn people in two minutes unless you can cure them.
- **Strangers at the door.** Let them in or turn them away; some help, some hide a bite.
- **The surface by day.** 13 searchable sites (wrecks, camps, ruins, caches) with loot and ambush risk, or send a survivor scavenging.
- **Nights you plan for.** Tonight's zombie routes are shown all day as red paths. Bend them with barricades; build a funnel of turrets, mines, spikes, flame and saws.
- **Six guns** from pistol to LMG, each upgradeable (damage, magazine, reload).
- **Built-in tutorial** that walks you through a first night, a visitor, an incident and the second-night horde.
